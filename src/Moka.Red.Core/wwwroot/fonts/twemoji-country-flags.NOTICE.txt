Twemoji Country Flags

twemoji-country-flags.woff2 is "TwemojiCountryFlags.woff2" from country-flag-emoji-polyfill 0.1.10 by TalkJS
(https://github.com/talkjs/country-flag-emoji-polyfill), shipped here unchanged. TalkJS made it as a subset of
Mozilla's Twemoji Mozilla font (https://github.com/mozilla/twemoji-colr), keeping only the flag glyphs.

The flag artwork is from Twemoji (https://github.com/twitter/twemoji).
